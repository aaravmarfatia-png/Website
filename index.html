How to install

First launch is unsigned, so your OS will ask you to confirm. Here's how.

Windows
  1. Download the .zip build kit and unzip it.
  2. Run the installer inside. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Follow the prompts to finish installing, then launch the app.
macOS
  1. Open the .dmg and drag the app into your Applications folder.
  2. The first time, right-click the app → Open, then confirm in the dialog. (A plain double-click may be blocked because the app is unsigned.)
  3. If macOS still blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

FAQ

Is it safe to install?
Yes. The app runs locally and only creates drafts in your own Gmail. You can verify each download against the SHA-256 checksum shown on the download card.
Why does my computer say it's unsigned?
Code-signing certificates cost money and require developer program enrollment. This app isn't signed yet, so Windows and macOS show a one-time warning. Follow the install steps above to approve it.
Does it send email automatically?
No. It only writes drafts to your Gmail Drafts folder. Nothing is sent unless you review and send it yourself.
What does it access?
It needs permission to create Gmail drafts on your account. It does not read your inbox contents beyond what's needed to build drafts, and your data stays on your machine.
